Help & Support

What is "Cash available"?

Your starting cash, plus all the money in you logged, minus expenses, stock purchases and draws. Log every day, and it matches the cash in your shop.

What is "Safe to draw"?

70% of your cash. The other 30% is kept as a buffer for emergencies, so drawing money — or spending on stock — never leaves you with nothing.

When is a product "Running low"?

When the number in your shop is at or below the "Running low at" number you set for that product.

What is a draw?

Money you take out of the shop for yourself or your family. It is not a shop expense, so ThathaCash keeps it separate.

I made a mistake

Go to Transactions, tap the entry, and delete it. Then log it again.
